- v1.3 local practice library remains unchanged before and after pressing Add starter sessions.
- Exactly 10 sessions / 30 practices added on first press, zero on second.
- A session can be opened; its total equals the target.
- Each practice has 2 frames and a usable diagram.

- Export PDF and save a GIF from a new practice on iPad.
- Turn off Wi-Fi after initial online load and reopen the installed app.
- Test Back up library and Import backup on an *isolated test browser*, not by overwriting your working device.
