# CoachTomAsh Studio v1.3.1 • Academy Starter Sessions

This update **merges 10 sample sessions and 30 example practices** into your existing CoachTomAsh Studio v1.3 library. No subscription, log-in or external service.

## Update GitHub Pages
1. Open the installed app and **Back up library** to iCloud Drive before updating.
2. Extract the update ZIP. Upload its three files **index.html**, **starter-sessions.js** and **service-worker.js** to the **root of your existing** `tomash-bit/CoachTomAsh-studio` GitHub repository on `main`. Replace the two matching old files and commit.
3. Once Pages publishes, open `https://tomash-bit.github.io/CoachTomAsh-studio/?v=131` in Safari while online. Open **Session builder**.
4. Press **+ Add 10 starter sessions** and confirm. This performs a non-destructive merge.
5. Choose one of the new sessions from **My saved sessions**. Check a practice in **My practice library**, edit or duplicate it, and use **Print / Save PDF** to create a session handout.
6. Reopen your Home Screen app and confirm the new sessions remain available offline.

## Notes
- The normal **Import backup** command remains destructive; do **not** use it for this starter pack.
- Tapping **Add 10 starter sessions** again skips the pack’s existing fixed IDs, preserving edits.
- Goals are placed beyond goal lines in the example practice diagrams.
- Every example drill has two editable frames and details for objective, organisation, constraints, coaching points, progressions and success criteria.
- Numbers, pitch dimensions, progression and intensity are **starting suggestions**, not verified club prescriptions; adapt to facility, player needs, medical status and practice design.
- You may use the included `CoachTomAsh_Starter_Session_Pack.json` to inspect or archive the source content, but it is **not** a full app backup and is not accepted by the old **Import backup** button.
